About WanderLux

A fictional travel concept built around thoughtful service and meaningful journeys.

Our story

Designed around real travellers.

WanderLux is a fictional new travel agency created for an ICT502 student web development project. Its service concept focuses on personalised holidays, guided tours and flexible packages.

Our mission is to make travel decisions simpler by offering clear information, thoughtful suggestions and a welcoming experience.

Our customer service values

Clarity, respect, responsiveness and inclusive travel planning guide every customer interaction.
